v1 is complete on the evidence in hand plus the low-cost additions (the growth cross-check, S9, S10,

## Register and format checks run before shipping
No em-dashes (the three-hyphen sequence and the unicode character), no banned words, no self-undermining
meta, no future pledges, no named politicians or parties (administrations by period, measures by
decree and date), critique by logic. Every debt and financing figure at both exchange-rate valuations.
Reduced-form status, the parallel-versus-official valuation, and the GDP rebase are reported as neutral
properties. The completed DSA is reused, not re-estimated; the numbers that changed from the DSA are
S6 (right-sized from illustrative 7.5 percent to a 4-to-12 percent range) and the new S9 and S10, each
flagged with its reason.
