# 01 Independent growth cross-check

Exhibit: `exhibits/fig_growth_crosscheck.png`.

Three independent instruments read Bolivia's near-term real activity. They are placed side by side for
the 2025 to 2026 window, each with its native scope.

| Instrument | Near-term read | Bracket | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| MEFP (program) | 2025 minus 1.6 (cited); 2026 about minus 1.0 (program-consistent) | n.a. | the program's own path |
| MFMod-BOL v5 | 2025 minus 3.3 | n.a. | independent structural model, pre-program vintage |
| ISAE (NO2 nowcast) | window minus 2.8 | fire-adjusted minus 1.4 to decree lower toward minus 6 | independent combustion nowcast, five-to-six-month lead, deflator-free |

## Reading
Two of the three independent instruments put the 2025 to 2026 contraction materially deeper than the
program. The structural model reads 2025 at minus 3.3 against the program's minus 1.6. The combustion
nowcast reads the September 2025 to August 2026 window at minus 2.8 central, against a
program-consistent 2026 near minus 1.0, and its bracket runs from minus 1.4 (adjusting for biomass-fire
NO2) down toward minus 6 (the decree lower bound). The program's near-term growth sits at the shallow
end of the independent range.

The same optimism is the natural prior on the program's recovery assumption. If the program reads the
near-term contraction shallower than two independent instruments, the burden of proof sits on its
assumed rebound to about 2.5 to 3 percent by 2029, which is the growth the debt path depends on
(`MFMOD_BOL/DSA/outputs/05_bifurcation.md`: the minimum sustained growth to hold the market-rate ratio
below 90 percent by 2029 is 1.7 percent). The cross-check does not re-estimate that recovery; it
records that the instrument which is most optimistic on the part that can be checked independently is
the same instrument whose recovery assumption cannot yet be checked.

## Scope discipline
The ISAE bracket is carried, not its point, wherever it feeds a debt path (`02_new_scenarios.md`, S9).
The ISAE is not extended past its horizon. The 2028-and-after path stays MEFP-based in every exhibit
and is flagged. No independent instrument in hand reaches the recovery horizon; that gap is a v2 input
(`04_v1_to_v2.md`).
