# 03 Distributional overlay: pending, direction stated

The MFMod-microsim engine and the most recent EH survey are not accessible for an end-to-end run in
this environment. The poverty and decile overlay is therefore recorded as pending, with the
transmission direction stated for each scenario and the magnitude deferred to the fuel-incidence work
order. No incidence numbers are invented.

## Transmission direction by scenario
| Scenario | Channel to households | Direction | Sharpest-hit group |
|---|---|---|---|
| S3 fuel cost-recovery | January-2027 cost-recovery pricing (MEFP p.6, para 13) and the large-consumer diesel differential (Bs 18 versus Bs 9.80, DS 5676) raise transport and food prices | regressive in the direct fuel budget share; mixed once transport pass-through is added | urban low-income with high transport shares; rural through freight and input costs |
| S5 depreciation | a further real depreciation raises the price of tradables and imported food | real-income loss concentrated in high imported-goods shares | urban households; partly offset in rural tradable-producing households |
| S7 combined | the deepest activity contraction, largest employment and labor-income channel | poverty headcount rises most in the trough year | informal and wage-employed in the combustion sectors |
| S9 ISAE baseline | the near-term contraction is deeper than the program assumes (minus 2.8 window), so the labor-income and employment channel is larger in 2026 | steeper near-term real-income loss than the program baseline | the same combustion-sector employment the ISAE tracks (about 40 percent of GDP) |

## What the overlay needs to become a result (v2)
The poverty headcount and the loss by decile, urban and rural, for S3, S5, S7, and S9 require the
macro-to-micro run: the occupational multinomial logit, the Mincer earnings equation, the reweighting
to population totals, the sector-output rescaling, and the remittance assignment, applied to the most
recent EH survey. The engine and the survey are the v2 inputs. The fuel-price incidence magnitude for
S3 is delegated to the fuel-incidence work order rather than re-derived here.
